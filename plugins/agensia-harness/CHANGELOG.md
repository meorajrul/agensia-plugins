# Agensia Harness changelog

## 1.2.0

Generic P2A method with G0–G8 design gates, harness levels, ontology packs, golden sets, saved Workflow specifications and a non-waivable production prohibition. Includes consultant mode for builders who are not the process owner: elicitation cards with provenance tags and owner playback, role-based naming with a role-binding source, a TOGAF ADM crosswalk, retrofit review of existing Pods, and templates for Pod Charter, SIPOC + RACI, Data and Connector Catalogue, role binding and Pod Contract. No platform behaviour or runtime certification is implied.
